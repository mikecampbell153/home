Our Lady of Perpetual Help Finance Committee
Summary of ArchIndy Norms

Canon Law

  • Canon 532 - The Pastor has full decision making authority over the affairs of the Parish.
  • Canon 537 - Each parish is to have a finance council to aid the pastor in the administration of parish goods.

Membership

  • Registered members of the parish that are skilled in
    • financial,
    • business or
    • legal matters.
  • Members are to represent the diversity of the parish and its ministries.
  • Three to seven members of the parish are to be appointed by the pastor.
  • The chairperson is also to be appointed by the pastor.
  • A member of the school commission is to be appointed by the pastor.
  • There are no term limits for continuity and institutional knowledge.
    • Members are expected to serve at least three years with six years or more preferred.
  • The chairperson will also serve as a member of the parish council.
  • Members of the finance committee may not have a conflict of interest due to relation, business
    affiliation, or other reason.

Responsibilities

  • To respond to requests for consultation from the pastor.
  • To coordinate the preparation of the annual parish budget.
  • To periodically evaluate the financial condition of the parish by reviewing financials.
  • To ensure that permanent, accurate and complete minutes.
  • To serve as a resource for the parish bookkeeper.
  • To be an integral part of the Parish Pastoral Council in financial matters.
  • To provide assistance to parish staff during the budgeting process and as other questions arise.
  • To ensure that annual budgets and annual financial reports are filed with the Office of Accounting Services.

Suggested Consultations

  • To evaluate extraordinary or unbudgeted expenditures.
  • To evaluate the potential addition or withdrawal of funds to or from a parish savings account,
    ADLF deposit account, or other investment account.
  • To facilitate a system of internal controls.
  • To ensure that the parish accounting system and chart of accounts are appropriate and are in
    compliance with those recommended by the Archdiocese.
  • To evaluate the appointment of a bookkeeper.
  • Mandatory: Any sale or lease of property over $500,000.
