Christian Anthopology, Papal Documents

  • Quemadmodum Deus – Blessed Pius IX, Decree of the Sacred Congregation of Rites, 1870
    Proclaimed St. Joseph as Patron of the Universal Church and raised his feast of March 19 to a solemnity
  • Quamquam Pluries (On Devotion to St. Joseph)
    Encyclical of Pope Leo XIII promulgated on August 15, 1889.
  • Redemptoris Custos (Guardian of the Redeemer)
    Apostolic Exhortation of Pope St. John Paul II On August 15, 1989
  • Patris Corde (With a Father's Heart)
    Apostolic Letter on St. Joseph as Patron of the Universal Church on December 8, 2020
