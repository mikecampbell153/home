The Apostles Creed

1. I believe in God, the Father Almighty, Creator of Heaven and Earth

  • Most Holy Trinity
  • Creation and Fall of Adam
  • Divine Revelation

2. And in Jesus Christ, His only Son, our Lord

  • Hypostatic Union

3. Who was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the Virgin Mary

  • Five Marian Dogmas
    • Perpetual Virginity
    • Mother of God
    • Immaculate Conception
    • Assumption
    • Mediatrix of all Grace (proposed)

4. Suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, died, and was buried

  • Holy Thursday
  • Good Friday
  • Eucharist
  • The Mass
  • Sorrowful Mysteries

5. He descended into hell, the third day He arose again from the dead

6. He ascended into Heaven, sits at the right hand of God, the Father Almighty

7. From there He will come to judge the living and the dead

  • Four Last Things (Death, Judgment, Heaven, and Hell)

8. I believe in the Holy Spirit

  • 7 Gifts of the Holy Spirit
  • Spoken through the prophets

9. The Holy Catholic Church; the Communion of Saints

  • Precepts (Mass on Sunday & Holy Days, Confession & Eucharist Annually, etc.)
  • Four Marks of the Church (One, Holy, Catholic, and Apostolic)
  • Liturgical Year
  • Pope, Bishops, Priests, Consecrated Life, and Laity

10. The forgiveness of sins

11. The resurrection of the body

12. Life everlasting
